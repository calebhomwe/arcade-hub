vs 1.e4
  - Petrov (Russian) Defense :: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nf6
    Challenge e4 instantly — solid symmetry avoids memorized Italian bombs.
  - Two Knights Defense :: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nf6 4.Ng5 d5!
    Return the gambit pawn for time; never fear Ng5 when d5 strikes back.
  - Caro-Kann Exchange Variation :: 1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.exd5 cxd5 4.c4 Nf6 5.Nc3 e6
    Trade in the center, then develop every minor piece before queens leave home.
  - French Advance :: 1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.e5 c5 4.c3 Nc6
    Anchor on e5, hit with ...c5 and ...f6 — your space gets undermined on purpose.
  - Sicilian Classical Setup :: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 d6
    Trade c-pawn for d-pawn pressure and counterattack the half-open c-file.

vs 1.d4
  - Queen's Gambit Accepted (active) :: 1.d4 d5 2.c4 dxc4 3.Nf3 Nf6 4.e3 e6 5.Bxc4 c5
    Give the pawn back at the right moment to free ...c5 counterplay.
  - Semi-Slav Meran Setup :: 1.d4 d5 2.c4 c6 3.Nf3 Nf6 4.Nc3 e6 5.e3 Nbd7
    A wall of pawns hides big break plans (...e5 or ...b5) depending on White's choice.
  - Tarrasch Defense :: 1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 c5
    Accept the isolated queen's pawn — piece activity beats structure here.
  - Benko Gambit Idea :: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 c5 3.d5 b5
    Trade wings for permanent open a/b-files the queen trains on forever.

as White

  - English Symmetrical Plan :: 1.c4 c5 2.Nf3 Nf6 3.g3 b6 4.Bg2 Bb7
    Flip-board patience — aim for d4 breaks while keeping central flexibility.
  - Catalan Intro :: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.g3 d5 4.Bg2 Be7 5.Nf3 O-O
