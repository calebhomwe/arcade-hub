[0] Scholar's Mate | Bishop's Opening | side=White
moves: e2e4 e7e5 f1c4 b8c6 d1h5 g8f6 h5f7
hook: Queen and bishop both stare at f7, the one square only the king defends.
punish: 4.Qxf7# - the four-move mate every beginner loses to exactly once.
avoid: Answer 3.Qh5 with 3...g6!. The queen has no good square and you gain time hitting her.

[1] Legal's Mate | Italian / Philidor | side=White
moves: e2e4 e7e5 g1f3 b8c6 f1c4 d7d6 b1c3 c8g4 f3e5 g4d1 c4f7 e8e7 c3d5
hook: White hangs the queen on purpose. Taking it is mate in two.
punish: 7.Nd5# - a mate delivered by two minor pieces while Black owns the queen.
avoid: After 5.Nxe5, play 5...Nxe5! and win a clean pawn instead of grabbing the queen.

[2] Blackburne Shilling Gambit | Italian Game | side=Black
moves: e2e4 e7e5 g1f3 b8c6 f1c4 c6d4 f3e5 d8g5 e5f7 g5g2 h1f1 g2e4 c4e2 d4f3
hook: 3...Nd4 offers the e5 pawn. Blackburne is said to have won a shilling a time with it.
punish: 7...Nf3# - a smothered-style mate with White's whole army still at home.
avoid: Meet 3...Nd4 with 4.Nxd4! exd4 5.O-O. White is simply better; the greedy 4.Nxe5 loses.

[3] Fried Liver Attack | Two Knights Defence | side=White
