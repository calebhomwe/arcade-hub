# Material textures (Block Blast, 2048, Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four, Minesweeper)

Seamless material photographs from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each was downsized to 512 px JPEG. They are drawn under coloured lacquer, enamel and stone
by `games/kit/mat.js`, and behind the parchment and wood panels of `games/kit/pz.js`.
Nothing is loaded from the network at run time.
